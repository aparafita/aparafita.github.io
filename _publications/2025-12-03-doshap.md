**Abstract**: Among explainability techniques, SHAP stands out as one of the most popular,
but often overlooks the causal structure of the problem. In response, do-SHAP
employs interventional queries, but its reliance on estimands hinders its practical
application. To address this problem, we propose the use of estimand-agnostic
approaches, which allow for the estimation of any identifiable query from a single
model, making do-SHAP feasible on complex graphs. We also develop a novel
algorithm to significantly accelerate its computation at a negligible cost, as well as
a method to explain inaccessible Data Generating Processes. We demonstrate the
estimation and computational performance of our approach, and validate it on two
real-world datasets, highlighting its potential in obtaining reliable explanations.
